# Proposed handwriting movements — all 29 letters

All routes are **PROPOSED: for teacher review**. The final guide paths are constrained to the printed glyph’s ink so the red route stays on the letter; this geometry adjustment does not establish or approve the movement order. The visual body is the exact Amiri Quran outline; the red movement guides are manually authored. A font does not establish a teaching stroke order, and the route is not the winding direction of its outline. The original physical card photographs were not supplied, so exact card matching is not claimed.

Jim’s top line uses the owner’s specified **left-to-right** convention. Common handwriting/calligraphy programmes can differ in entry, continuity and pen lifts, particularly Jim/Ha/Kho, Ain/Ghain, Sod/Dhod, Tho/Zho, isolated Ha and Kaf’s internal mark. Teachers should select the programme’s consistent construction before connecting these routes to tracing scoring. Dot groups are assigned the last numbered movement; each red point identifies a dot location, not a direction to move the dot.
