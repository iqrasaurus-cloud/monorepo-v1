====================================================================
IQRASAURUS — PROGRAMME PAGE CONTENT TEMPLATE
====================================================================
One copy per programme. Fill in your own text where you see [ ... ].
Every [ ] maps to a field in that programme's content.ts, so a filled-in
copy can be transcribed straight across. Based on the five-section
Quran Investigators page (BUILD_SPEC.md section 6B).

Before you write (see CLAUDE.md for the full rules):
  - Never write, translate or complete Quranic Arabic or hadith text
    yourself. Paste only what you already have, exactly as written.
  - No invented quotes, statistics or testimonials. If you don't have
    a real one yet, leave the line blank. Blank lines and anything
    marked [[CONTENT NEEDED]] are hidden on the live site.
  - Write concretely: what a child does, what a parent needs, who to
    contact. Short sentences. No slogans in capitals.
  - The credential line is fixed and added automatically; don't
    reword it: "Led by ARS-recognised asatizah and operated as an
    IECP in Singapore."
  - Spell the brand IqraSaurus.
